Blkuzz Privacy Policy

Last updated 29.07.26

Welcome to Blkuzz, a platform created for creatives who want to collaborate, connect, and work on developing passion projects.

At Blkuzz, your privacy comes first. We do everything in our power to ensure that your content stays secure and your lines of communication remain private. Our aim is to empower you to create freely, confidently, and without worry.

By using Blkuzz, you agree to this Privacy Policy, which explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Who We Are

Blkuzz is operated by BLKUZZ LTD.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at:
info@blkuzz.com

The Data We Collect

We collect only what's needed to provide and improve the Blkuzz experience:

  • Account information: your name, email, password (we do not see your password), and profile details (creative tags, bio, links, etc.)
  • User content: anything you upload, text, images, videos, audio, or project posts.
  • Communication data: messages, collaborations, and interactions between members (kept private and encrypted wherever possible).
  • Analytics: how you use the site, includes but not limited to, pages visited, time spent, and potentially technical data like IP address or device type (used for performance and security).

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. Ever.

How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us to:

  • Run and maintain your Blkuzz. Your data gives us insight into what is working and what isn't, along with feedback we will request from you regularly.
  • Connect you with other creatives and potential collaborators.
  • Improve platform performance and fix bugs.
  • Feature your work in Spotlights or promotional materials.
  • Keep our community safe and prevent misuse.

We may occasionally send you updates or alerts about Blkuzz to which you can opt out anytime.

Protecting Your Content and Communication

  • We use industry standard security measures (encryption, password hashing, and secure servers) to protect all data.
  • Private messages are not shared with third parties unless legally required.
  • You control your own content, you can edit or delete it at any time.
  • If we ever detect unusual activity or data risks, we act immediately to secure your account and notify you.

Cookies & Analytics

Blkuzz uses cookies and analytics tools to improve performance and understand how people use the platform. These tools help us fix issues and tailor features. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

Sharing Information

We only share data when necessary:

  • With trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, or communication tools) who follow strict confidentiality and security standards.
  • When required by law or to prevent fraud or abuse.

We do not share or sell user lists to advertisers or external marketers.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Access or request a copy of your data.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for email communication.

Contact info@blkuzz.com for any of the above requests.

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide the Blkuzz service or meet legal obligations.

When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

Blkuzz is designed for adults (18+).

We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this policy to reflect improvements or legal changes.

If we make major updates, we'll notify you directly through the platform or by email.
