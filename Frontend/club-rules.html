Blkuzz Club Rules

Updated 29.07.26

  1. Treat members with kindness and respect.
  2. Show up for your collaborators.
  3. Take part.
  4. Keep all feedback constructive.
  5. Shout about the work you're doing.
  6. Never copy, or take credit for another member's work.
  7. Contact headquarters directly if you need special assistance.
  8. Never request money from another member. Collaboration is free.
